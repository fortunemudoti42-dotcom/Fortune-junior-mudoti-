About Fortune

Fortune Junior Mudoti is a South African footballer who plays as a centre-forward. His football journey includes representing Sakumlandela Primary School in local tournament football. He is currently continuing his education while keeping his football journey open. His focus is on balancing school, personal development and his love of the game.
